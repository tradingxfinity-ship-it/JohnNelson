San Antonio is one of the country's biggest military communities, home to Joint Base San Antonio's locations at Fort Sam Houston, Lackland, and Randolph. If you've got orders here, buying a home can make sense, but military moves run on tight timelines. A little planning goes a long way.

## Start from your report date

Work backward from the date you need to be here. Many buyers aim to close on or shortly after arrival, so they can move straight from temporary lodging into the new home. That usually means starting your search a couple of months ahead, even from out of state.

## Get your VA loan lined up early

If you're eligible, a VA home loan can be a major advantage. Eligible borrowers can often buy with no down payment, though a VA funding fee usually applies unless you're exempt. A few steps to take early:

1. **Request your Certificate of Eligibility** (your lender can often get it for you).
2. **Talk to a lender experienced with VA loans** and get preapproved, so you know your budget before you shop.
3. **Plan for the VA appraisal,** which checks both value and basic property standards. Homes that need significant repairs can run into trouble, so it helps to know this before you make an offer.

## Choose the right part of town

Commute matters. San Antonio's bases are spread across the city, and traffic can turn a short distance into a long drive. Think about:

- Your daily route to base, and your spouse's commute if there is one.
- Schools, if you have children.
- Whether you might rent the home out at your next PCS, which makes resale and rental demand worth considering now.

## Shopping from out of state

You don't have to be here to start. Video walkthroughs, detailed photos, and a local team who can look at homes in person make remote buying much more manageable. Many military families make an offer before they arrive.
